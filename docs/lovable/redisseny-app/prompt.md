Redesign the screens of **Shine**, a mobile-first app (iOS, Android and web) that trains reading-related skills in children with dyslexia. Build a clickable, frontend-only prototype with mock data in TypeScript (no backend, no auth, no database). All visible copy in **Catalan**, as in the attached screenshots.

The 10 attached screenshots are the CURRENT app. Keep its information and flows, but change the visual language and fix the usability problems listed below. The goal is a look that is clearly Shine's own and does not read as "an app generated by AI".

## 1. Who uses it and how

- An adult (parent, therapist or teacher, the "titular") and a child (the "alumne") use the app **together, side by side**. The adult picks the child, launches the session and reads the instructions; the child plays. Do not add "requires an adult" flags: every screen assumes both are there.
- Two registers in one app: **tutor screens** (choose learner, home, progress, settings) are calm and informative; **child screens** (the activity player) are big, tactile and playful. Make the switch between both registers visible (for example, the player takes the whole screen with its own background and typography scale).
- Users have dyslexia: never justified text, line height at least 1.6, slightly open letter spacing, body text at least 18 px, touch targets at least 48 px, WCAG AA contrast. Mistakes are never punished: no red for the child's answers, no "wrong" sounds or shakes; an error is just "torna-ho a provar".

## 2. Domain (use these names and data in the mocks)

- **Six skill areas**, each a vertex of the progress map, each with a level from 0 to 10: Llenguatge i fonologia, Memòria de treball, Discriminació visual, Velocitat de processament, Atenció, Funcions executives.
- **Session**: 3 to 5 short activities chosen by the server. Kinds: regular session; evaluation session ("Sessió per conèixer-te", used at the start to place the child's levels); adjustment session, where after each activity the child answers "Com ha anat?" with three options (fàcil, bé, difícil). At the end of every session there is a short self-assessment: which activity was easiest, which was hardest, and how it went overall.
- **Nine activity types** (each needs its own player screen layout; give each type its own icon and accent so it is recognisable at a glance in lists):
  1. Parpelleig: one word appears and disappears every N seconds; the child reads it aloud.
  2. Llista vertical: words one by one, tap to advance.
  3. Lectura en veu alta: one paragraph of 35 to 80 words with a stopwatch.
  4. Posició d'imatges: pick the image whose name matches.
  5. Matriu de lletres: grid of letters, find all the targets (for example "Troba totes les p" among p, q, s, o), then "Comprovar"; 2 rounds.
  6. Ordenar síl·labes: drag syllables to build the word for an image.
  7. Segmentar paraules: split a run of letters into words.
  8. Repetició ràpida: read a list of words as fast as possible; the metric is rhythm, not correctness.
  9. Seqüència amb regla: stimuli appear one after another; tap only when the rule is met (attention test, fast pace).
- **Learners**: one adult can have several learners, each with a working language (CA or ES), an avatar from a fixed set of emojis, and a role (owner, guardian). Example data: Marta (ES, owner), Pau (CA, owner, active).
- Streak ("2 dies seguits"), activities and minutes this week, total attempts.

## 4. Usability problems in the current screens (fix them)

- Inici: two stat tiles and a streak card fill the first fold; the main action, "Continuar la sessió", is below the fold. The next session must be the first and largest thing on screen, with one primary action.
- Inici: the list of past sessions repeats titles ("Sessió per conèixer-te" three times) and keeps completed sessions as big cards. Replace it with a compact **path or timeline** of sessions (completed, current, next), Duolingo style but without lives, leagues or penalties.
- Sessió: activity titles are truncated ("Troba totes les p"), area chips wrap into two lines and compete with the progress bar. Show the activities as a short vertical sequence of steps, each with its type icon, its area colour and its state.
- Player, Matriu: on small screens "Comprovar" falls below the fold; selected cells differ only by a pale tint. Fit the grid to the viewport, keep the action fixed at the bottom, and mark selection with shape and border too (not colour only).
- Activity completed: after every single activity there is a full screen with a check icon, a stats card, a motivational card and two buttons. This interrupts the flow of a 5 minute session. Make it a brief inline celebration (1 to 2 seconds, respecting prefers-reduced-motion) that leads to the next activity, and move the detailed results to the session recap.
- El meu progrés: three identical stat tiles and a generic radar chart. Replace with a **skills map** (Peak "brainmap" idea): the six areas, each with its colour, its level 0 to 10 and its recent trend, readable without colour.
- Selector and Configuració: every learner is the same teal circle with an initial. Use the emoji avatar and a per-learner tint so the child recognises themself. Separate "Alumnes" (people) from "Preferències de lectura" (font, size, spacing) and "Compte".
- Reading preferences are hidden in a small link "Ajustar com es veuen les lletres" inside the player. Offer them as a visible control in Configuració and as a quick toggle in the player (OpenDyslexic, text size, letter spacing, word highlighting as in Speechify).

## 5. Visual direction: make it Shine, not generic AI

Avoid these patterns, which the current screens and most AI-generated apps share:

- Stat tiles with an icon inside a pale rounded square, repeated in a row.
- Cards with a coloured left border used for every message.
- Everything as white rounded cards with the same soft shadow on an off-white background.
- Waving-hand emoji greetings, sparkles icons, generic check-in-a-circle success screens, confetti.
- Teal-to-purple gradients, glassmorphism, blurred blobs, Inter or Nunito as the only typeface, shadcn default look.
- Pill chips for every tag.

Use instead (from our references: Elevate, Peak, Duolingo ABC, Speechify, Readability, CapWords):

- **One colour and one simple glyph per skill area** (six areas), used consistently in the session path, the activity steps, the player background accent and the skills map (Elevate gives each game its own identity). Choose a palette that is warm and distinct, with AA contrast on its own background; keep a calm neutral base (paper-like, not pure white) for tutor screens.
- **Typography with character but built for reading**: for example Lexend or Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for UI and reading text, with OpenDyslexic as a user option. Large, confident numbers for levels and times. No font below 14 px.
- **Shapes from reading and writing**: lined-paper rhythms, letter tiles with physical depth (pressed and raised states), a "bookmark" or "ribbon" motif for the current session instead of generic badges. Tactile, almost physical controls in the player (CapWords).
- **Session as a short path**: 3 to 5 steps drawn as a path, the current one raised; the recap closes the path.
- **Player backgrounds by area**, and a dark high-focus variant for fast attention tasks (Peak), with the same tokens.
- **Feedback**: immediate, gentle, with an optional sound toggle; correct answers grow or settle into place; errors simply soften and invite a retry.
- Motion: short (150 to 300 ms), purposeful, disabled with prefers-reduced-motion.
- Tutor information (Readability's parent reports): in Progrés, a short plain-language summary for the adult ("Punt fort: memòria de treball. Per continuar practicant: velocitat de processament").

## 6. Technical notes

We will port these tokens to React Native, so keep components simple (no heavy shadcn styling, no complex CSS effects that cannot be reproduced on mobile).
- Mock data in one file (`src/data/mock.ts`).
- Keep the 2-item bottom navigation (Inici, El meu progrés) on tutor screens; the player hides it.
